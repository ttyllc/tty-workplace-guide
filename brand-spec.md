# Brand Specification: Tech To You

**Visual Direction:** Modern Minimal (Linear/Vercel style)
**Tone:** Professional, Trustworthy, Efficient, High-End.

## Color Tokens (OKLch)
- `--bg`: oklch(99% 0.002 240) - Soft off-white/blue background
- `--surface`: oklch(100% 0 0) - Pure white for cards and containers
- `--fg`: oklch(18% 0.012 250) - Deep navy/slate for primary text
- `--muted`: oklch(54% 0.012 250) - Medium slate for secondary text
- `--border`: oklch(92% 0.005 250) - Subtle border for separation
- `--accent`: oklch(58% 0.18 255) - Trust-inducing professional blue

## Typography
- **Display:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'SF Pro Display', system-ui, sans-serif
- **Body:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'SF Pro Text', system-ui, sans-serif
- **Mono:** ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Menlo, Monaco, Consolas, monospace

## Design Rules
1. **Precision:** Tight letter-spacing on headings, hairline borders, and generous whitespace.
2. **Restraint:** Use `--accent` sparingly for primary CTAs and key highlights.
3. **Clarity:** Benefit-first language. No technical jargon without a plain-English explanation.
4. **Contrast:** Maintain high contrast for readability, especially in interaction states.
